Show every indicator line at the hovered bar in a chart's tooltip, so overlays read against the candle, and title daily bars with the date only. Thresholds and hidden `none` series stay out of the tooltip. The demo app now shows tooltips by default for every visitor who has not switched them off themselves. A visitor who switched them off before this release gets them on once and can switch them off again.
